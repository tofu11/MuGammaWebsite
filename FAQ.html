What is Theta Tau?

Theta Tau is a co-ed professional engineering fraternity that promotes the social and professional development of its members.

How can I join?

You can join Theta Tau by participating in our RUSH events at the beginning of each semester and following our recruitment process.

What majors are accepted?

Theta Tau is open to all engineering majors, including Computer Science, Civil Engineering, and Aerospace.
