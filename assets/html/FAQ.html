Comprised of a large, diverse group of engineers with shared goals, Theta Tau is a co-ed professional engineering fraternity committed to helping its members grow both as engineers and individuals. It fosters the social and professional development of all its members.
You can join Theta Tau by participating in our RUSH events at the beginning of each semester and by following our recruitment process.
Rush takes place during the first few weeks of each semester and consists of several events, giving you the opportunity to meet the brothers and learn about the values our fraternity upholds. It's a chance for you to connect with brothers and for us to find motivated engineers. At the end of Rush, bids and interviews will be extended to a select group of individuals, who can choose to accept or decline the bid and begin pledging. Rush is completely free of charge and non-binding.
Theta Tau is open to all full-time students majoring in an Engineering related curriculum, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Aerospace.
A bid is the formal invitation you need to begin the pledge process!
To qualify for a bid, you must attend at least three rush events. However, we recommend attending as many events as possible. This gives you the chance to meet more brothers and for them to get to know you better. Ultimately, securing a bid depends on demonstrating the key pillars we value: service, professionalism, and brotherhood.
Pledging takes place over the course of the semester and is led by selected brothers who are there to guide and support you. Their goal is to help you grow and embody the core pillars that Theta Tau values: service, professionalism, and brotherhood.
For any more questions, you can contact us here at mgthetatau@gmail.com!
