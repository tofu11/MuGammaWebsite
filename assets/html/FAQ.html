Comprised of a large diverse group of engineers with similar goals committede to helping each other grow as engineers and people, Theta Tau is a co-ed professional engineering fraternity that promotes the social and professional development of its members.
You can join Theta Tau by participating in our RUSH events at the beginning of each semester and following our recruitment process.
Rush occurs in the beginning weeks of each semester, consisting of multiple events, allowing you to meet the brothers and see how our fraternity stands for. It is an opporunity for you to meet brothers, and for us to find other motivated engineers. At the end of rush, bids and interviews will be offered to a limited amount of people, those of which may accept or decline the bid and begin pledging. Rush is completely free of charge and non-binding.
Theta Tau is open to all full-time students majoring in engineering, including Computer Science, Civil Engineering, and Aerospace.
A bid is the formal invitation you need to begin the pledge process!
To qualify for a bid, you must attend at least three rush events. However, we recommend attending as many events as possible. This gives you the chance to meet more brothers and for them to get to know you better. Ultimately, securing a bid depends on demonstrating the key pillars we value: service, professionalism, and brotherhood.
Pledging takes place over the course of the semester and is led by selected brothers who are there to guide and support you. Their goal is to help you grow and embody the core pillars that Theta Tau values: service, professionalism, and brotherhood.
For any more questions, you can contact us here at mgthetatau@gmail.com!
